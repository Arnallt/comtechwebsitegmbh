## Entity separation

ComTech GmbH is a Swiss technology company. It shares ownership with a separate
Dubai-based gold-token issuer, which is a distinct legal entity. ComTech GmbH
performs no issuance, custody, trading or redemption for that entity or any
other. The exact wording of this separation is with counsel — see the scope
statement above.
